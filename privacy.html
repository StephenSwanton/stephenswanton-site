Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This website, stephenswanton.realtor, is operated by Stephen Swanton, a licensed real estate salesperson affiliated with Coldwell Banker St. Croix Realty, through his company VI Real Property Solutions, LLC ("I," "me," or "my"). This policy explains what information I collect when you use this website or contact me, how I use it, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, the type of property you are interested in, areas of interest, price range, timeframe, a property address, and any message you send.

Like most websites, this site and the services that support it may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

How I use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the real estate services you request.
  • To send you information related to your inquiry, such as property details, market information and appointment scheduling.
  • To contact you by phone, email or text message when you have given permission to do so.
  • To keep records of our communications and comply with legal and brokerage requirements.
  • To maintain and improve this website.

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers that help me deliver messages (for example, my messaging platform and phone carriers), and only for that purpose.

If you agree to receive text messages, you can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See the Terms for full messaging terms.

How I share information

I do not sell your personal information. I may share it only:

  • With Coldwell Banker St. Croix Realty, my brokerage, as needed to provide real estate services and meet brokerage and recordkeeping requirements.
  • With service providers that support my business, such as my customer relationship management, email and website hosting platforms, who may use it only to provide services to me.
  • With other parties to a real estate transaction (for example, a lender, closing attorney or title company) when you ask me to, or when it is necessary to complete your transaction.
  • When required by law, regulation or legal process.

Email

If you submit a form, you may receive a confirmation email and follow-up emails about your inquiry. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email.

Cookies and third-party services

This site uses third-party services, including embedded forms provided by my customer relationship management platform, which may use cookies or similar technologies to function and to measure form submissions. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data security and retention

I use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. I keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described above and as required by law or brokerage recordkeeping rules.

Your choices

You may ask me to access, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by using the contact information below. Some records may need to be kept to meet legal or brokerage requirements.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
