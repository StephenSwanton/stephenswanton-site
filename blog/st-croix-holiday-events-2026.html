St. Croix Holiday Season 2026: Jump Up, the Lantern Parade and More

By Stephen Swanton, REALTOR®, Coldwell Banker St. Croix Realty
November 25, 2026

One of my favorite things about living on St. Croix is how the whole island comes out for the holidays. If you are visiting, house hunting, or already call the island home, here is what is on the calendar between now and mid-December.

Friday, November 27: Christiansted Jump Up

The day after Thanksgiving, downtown Christiansted turns into a street party from 6 to 10 pm, with live music, moko jumbies, local food and shops open late. If you are on island looking at property, it is a great way to see the town at its liveliest.

Saturday, November 28: Cultural Downtown Window Reveal

Purple Papaya hosts its cultural holiday window reveal downtown, one of several storefront window unveilings that kick off the season in Christiansted.

Saturday, December 5: Lantern Parade, Lighting of the Ruins and Sunday-Market Tree Lighting

A full evening from 5:30 pm to midnight. The mobile parade lines up at 5:30 and steps off around 6, with the ruins lit along the route. The pedestrian lantern parade starts at 7:30 pm at the Bassin Triangle and winds along King, Prince, Company and Market Streets to Sunday-Market Square, where vendors and entertainment run from 5 pm to midnight and the tree is lit at 8:30. The Sunday-Market display stays up through January 9.

Wednesday, December 9: Gallows Bay Holiday Event

Gallows Bay celebrates from 4 to 7 pm, a nice evening to stroll the shops and waterfront.

Friday, December 11: Ten Sleepless Knights Park Tree Lighting

The 8th annual tree lighting starts at 6:30 pm, with the program at 7 and a community countdown at 8:30. The tree stays lit through January 9.

Saturday, December 12: Christiansted Boat Parade

Decorated boats light up Christiansted harbor. The boardwalk fills early, so plan to arrive ahead of time for a good view.

Art Thursday

Art Thursday, the Christiansted gallery walk, is generally held on the third Thursday of the month from November through May, with galleries and studios open in the evening. The 2026 to 2027 season dates are still being announced, so check GoToStCroix's Art Thursday page before you head downtown.

Dates and times can change, so confirm with the organizers before you go. For more island events, the GoToStCroix events calendar is a great resource. And if you are coming down to look at property this season, let me know. I am always happy to point you toward a good evening out between showings.
