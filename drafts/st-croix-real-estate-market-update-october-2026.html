Every month I pull the numbers from our MLS so buyers and sellers can see what is actually happening on St. Croix, not just what the headlines say. Here is where things stood at the end of October 2026, with the past twelve months (November 2025 through October 2026) for context.

Homes

There were 158 homes for sale on St. Croix at the end of October, close to the 163 listed a year earlier. Over the past twelve months, 176 homes sold, or roughly 15 a month. At that pace, today's inventory represents about 11 months of supply, which still gives buyers real choice.

The median asking price of active homes was $598,500, down from $799,000 a year ago. Part of that change reflects which homes are on the market right now, so I would read it as more options in the middle of the market rather than a straight price drop.

The encouraging number is speed. Homes that sold in October averaged 115 days on market, compared with about 241 days for homes that sold in November 2025. Another 13 homes went under contract in October. Well-priced, well-presented homes are moving.

Condos

Condo inventory has grown. There were 129 condos for sale at the end of October, up from 96 a year earlier, with a median asking price of $299,000. Over the past twelve months, 94 condos sold. Ten condos went under contract in October, the most in a single month since April.

With roughly 16 months of supply, condo buyers have room to compare complexes, HOA fees, insurance and rental rules. For condo sellers, pricing and presentation matter more than ever.

Land

Land remains the deepest part of the St. Croix market, with 361 lots and acreage listings at the end of October, down from 392 a year earlier. 125 land parcels sold over the past twelve months, and October had 16 land sales, the second-highest month of the year. Land buyers have plenty to choose from, and due diligence on zoning, access, utilities and coastal rules is where a good purchase is made.

What this means

For buyers, this is a market with selection, especially in condos and land, and room to negotiate on properties that have been sitting. For sellers, the October numbers show that homes priced right from day one are selling faster than they were a year ago.
