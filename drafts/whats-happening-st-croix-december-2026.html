December is my favorite month on island. The breeze picks up, downtown gets lit up, and it feels like everyone comes out at once. Here are my picks for the month ahead.

Fri
Nov 27

Christiansted Jump Up

6 to 10 pm downtown. The unofficial kickoff to the season: music, food and shops open late.

Fri
Dec 4

Garden of Lights opens

St. George Village Botanical Garden, 6 to 10 pm. Open Friday to Sunday evenings through December 27.

Fri
Dec 4

Wine in the Warehouse

6 to 9 pm at Bohlke International Aviation. The St. Croix Foundation's evening of wine, chef-prepared food and live music.

Sat
Dec 5

Lantern Parade and Lighting of the Ruins

The big one in Christiansted. Parade steps off around 6, the lantern walk starts at 7:30 at the Bassin Triangle, and the Sunday-Market tree lights at 8:30.

Sun
Dec 6

Crucian Coconut Festival

The 10th annual, 11 am to 6 pm at the Bethlehem Sugar Factory. Earlier that morning, the Reindeer Run Half Marathon and 5K starts at 7 at Cane Bay.

Wed
Dec 9

Gallows Bay Holiday Festival

4 to 7 pm. An easy evening along the Gallows Bay shops and waterfront.

Fri
Dec 11

Tree lighting and a movie at the fort

The Ten Sleepless Knights Park tree lighting starts at 6:30, and "Elf" screens at 6 on the lawn at Fort Christiansvaern. Bring a chair.

Sat
Dec 12

Christiansted Christmas Boat Parade

Boats from 3 pm, lit up after dark, with fireworks to finish. Get to the boardwalk early.

Thu
Dec 17

Art Thursday

From 5 pm in Christiansted. Galleries, shops and jewelry stores open late with local art and live music.

Sat
Dec 19

Coquito Festival

At The Chicken Shack. A whole festival for the island's favorite holiday drink.

From
Dec 26

Crucian Christmas Festival

The Village opens December 26, with boat races on the 27th, the Food Fair on the 30th, J'Ouvert on the 31st and the parades on January 1 and 2.

Dates and times can change, so double check before you head out. The GoToStCroix December calendar has the full list.

Coming down to look at property this month? Let me know. A showing in the afternoon and the Lantern Parade that evening is a pretty good day on St. Croix.
