December is my favorite month on island. The breeze picks up, downtown gets lit up, and it feels like everyone comes out at once. Here are my picks for the month ahead.

Fri
Nov 27

Christiansted Jump Up

6 to 10 pm downtown. The unofficial kickoff to the season: music, food and shops open late.

Sat
Dec 5

Lantern Parade and Lighting of the Ruins

The big one. Parade steps off around 6, the lantern walk starts at 7:30 at the Bassin Triangle, and the Sunday-Market tree lights at 8:30.

Wed
Dec 9

Gallows Bay Holiday Event

4 to 7 pm. An easy evening along the Gallows Bay shops and waterfront.

Fri
Dec 11

Ten Sleepless Knights Park Tree Lighting

Starts at 6:30, with the countdown and lighting at 8:30.

Sat
Dec 12

Christiansted Boat Parade

Lit-up boats on the harbor. Get to the boardwalk early.

Dates and times can change, so double check before you head out. The GoToStCroix calendar is a good place to confirm.

Coming down to look at property this month? Let me know. A showing in the afternoon and the Lantern Parade that evening is a pretty good day on St. Croix.
