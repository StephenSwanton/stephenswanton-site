About Stephen

Race-day discipline, applied to real estate

Stephen Swanton is a St. Croix real estate agent and ABR, PSA, and CLHMS-designated Realtor with Coldwell Banker St. Croix Realty, who channels the discipline of elite triathlon racing and his past career curating wine selections for Sonoma and Napa Michelin-starred restaurants into a refined, data-driven approach to real estate. He treats every transaction the way he treats race day: showing up early, putting in the time on the detailed work most agents skip, and knowing the terrain, literally and figuratively, better than anyone else on the course. Practicing real estate on St. Croix since 2018, his production has climbed through Coldwell Banker recognition tiers every year, from International President's Premier to Chairman's Club to International President's Elite to International President's Circle, and now the International Diamond Society.

Stephen has worked deliberately to become fluent in every corner of St. Croix real estate, so he can be your REALTOR® through every phase of your journey here, not just the transaction in front of you today. That might mean guiding a first-time buyer from raw land toward their first home, or later helping that same client move from leasing a commercial storefront into owning the property outright. It might mean walking an investor through not just buying their first rental property but successfully managing it long after closing, since Stephen's practice includes long-term rental property management alongside sales. Or it might mean helping a luxury buyer choose a waterfront estate with an eye toward which improvements will matter most whenever they decide to sell. That range, residential, luxury and waterfront, land, condos, commercial, and property management, is what lets Stephen tell a client not just what fits today but what the smart next step looks like.

Real estate runs in his family; he grew up rehabbing homes alongside his father, who has held a license of his own for years. Outside the office, Stephen serves as President of the Virgin Islands Triathlon Federation, a role he has built toward since joining the board in 2015, and as President-Elect of the St. Croix Board of Realtors. He is a World Triathlon Level 2 Certified Coach who mentors junior athletes, has raced at the World Triathlon Age-Group Championships in Rotterdam and Torremolinos-Andalucia, and the Ironman 70.3 World Championship in Nice. He also founded Virgin Islands Kombucha, the territory's first commercial kombucha brewery.
